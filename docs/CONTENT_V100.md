# Dustbound 0.10.0 content

The catalog contains 2,368 item IDs, including consumables and earlier special equipment. This release adds 96 cultural armor variants and 36 personal elite items. Existing weapon types have been visually revised without renumbering old items.

## Weapons

All 38 types have 24 material/quality combinations and 12 different constructions, with two quality finishes for each construction. This is 912 ordinary weapon variants. Length, width, curvature and class-appropriate fittings distinguish constructions; palette and surface finish also vary. Melee reach and attack delay reflect construction. The source remains 36 painted weapon families plus the rapier drawing; the constructions are procedural variations, not separately painted models.

Katana, wakizashi, nodachi, tanto, naginata, yari, nagamaki, kusarigama, frontier sabre, bowie knife, trail machete, butcher cleaver, war pick, boarding axe, kanabo, tetsubo, quarterstaff, hook blade, ring sabre, broken falchion, horse cutter, saw sword, twin sickle, spiked club, scout bow, repeater bow, heavy arbalest, hand crossbow, longbow, ranger rifle, dragoon pistol, scattergun, greatsword, railway maul, crescent glaive, anchor cleaver, scimitar and rapier.

## Settlements and armor

Trade boroughs retain practical market streets. Shogunate settlements use fortress courts and walled axes, monastery settlements use cloisters, caravan villages use uneven cottage/shack arrangements, Black Mesa uses a workshop cluster, and hostile camps have an open cooking area. Cultures remain recognizable after occupation. Dusk Haven retains its familiar starter layout.

Court brigandine, reed carapace, basalt plate and censor vestments each have 24 variants. They favor balanced protection, light stealth, heavy protection and weather sealing respectively. Regional shops stock the relevant profiles, and the equipment forge can make them. The new profiles reuse existing painted garment families.
