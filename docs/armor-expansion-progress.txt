Dust 1.0.2 armor expansion — review candidate

Thirty distinct sets: five recovered and twenty-five newly generated. Fifteen
of the new twenty-five are heavy armor. All new outfits have separate upper
arm and forearm art. Pieces use actual catalog items and ordinary equip/loot
logic, with weight, protection, movement, stealth and weather tradeoffs.

Rendering changes
- Near shoulder draws in front of the torso without a torso repaint over it.
- Far arm draws before trousers, preserving rear-limb occlusion.
- Narrower thighs/shins and a textured hip band keep both legs connected.
- Individual head fitting corrects hood and cap placement.
- Standing, combat, prone and crawling paths share the new artwork.

Army uniforms (soldier / veteran / officer)
Compact: Road Warden / Brass Sentinel / Charter Marshal.
Shogunate: Reed Ashigaru / Gate Lamellar / Steel Retainer.
Ash order: Ember Guard / Censor Carapace / Winter Sohei.

Both army directors and town guard outfitting use the same rank mapping.
Independent wanderers use faction/strength-appropriate pools; four legendary
sets retain their named owners and existing unique body-item IDs. Service
staff retain their work clothes. New smith stock rotates existing clothing
slots to preserve settlement stock budgets.

Save compatibility
All existing item IDs and v29 fields retain their offsets. New items and
merchant stock are appended in save v30. Existing runs upgrade NPC clothing
once, preserving party gear and dead, unconscious or stripped characters.

Reproduction
python3 scripts/pack_armor_sets.py
make -f scripts/native_v1000.mk -j4
python3 scripts/validate_v1000.py
make -j4  # PSPDEV must point to a PSPDEV installation
python3 scripts/emulator_v102.py /path/to/extracted/PPSSPP/AppDir

Native outfit review: tests/review_armor_sets.c includes render.c; link native
core objects excluding render.o and native_main.o. Captures include actual
inventory/world screens, mirrored passive/fight poses, two-handed contact,
prone/crawling poses and the three faction rank ladders.

Validation at checkpoint: complete source artwork and PSP compilation;
new shoulder/occlusion/connected-hip/uniform/save tests pass. Broader native
regressions and exact-executable PPSSPP qualification are in progress.
