# Dust 1.2.3 — Shoulder rest

Right-facing boots now align with the legs while standing and walking.

The passive greatsword rests on the shoulder at 45 degrees, held by the front
hand with an approximately 90-degree elbow. The rear arm hangs naturally and
swings during walking. The painted blade edge, rather than its center line,
rests above the shoulder cap so broad and narrow blades share the same pose.
The carry blends into the existing two-handed combat guard and attack poses.

The approved pose is covered across 21 greatswords, five species, three body
builds and both facings, including one-arm fallbacks. The release checks also
cover the historical native suites, 600 painted-face checks, and PSP emulator
scenarios for combat, walking, inventory, crafting, quests, storage, stolen
item expiry and expedition interiors. Physical PSP hardware was not tested.

Existing expeditions remain compatible with save format 36. Replace EBOOT.PBP
in your existing PSP/GAME/DUSTBOUND folder and keep your save files. The PSP ZIP
contains the same executable and installation layout.
