# Dust 1.2.1 — Field repairs

Character rendering now uses the same normalized skin colors for the face, neck, exposed arms and hands. Hair and beards follow the scalp and jaw rather than the neck; eight beard styles are available, and wilderness NPCs receive varied facial hair. Backpacks are smaller and mounted at the belt/upper back. Generated boots follow their source toe direction; the Iron Labourer cap is explicitly mirrored to match the wearer. Walking legs have fixed bone lengths. Katanas use a higher guard and greatswords a steeper shoulder carry. Unrelated procedural fittings have been removed from painted weapons, including boarding axes.

Read a full work offer before accepting it. Declining leaves the posting available and changes no money, inventory or active contracts. An accepted contract can be abandoned from its Journal note with Triangle. Abandoned work goes into the history. Escort/patrol assignments are released; an unfinished dispatch is removed. Freight deposits are forfeited on abandonment, as stated in the offer.

Camp supplies are taken from a physical supply chest, one item at a time. Opening it does not steal the whole cache. Camp beds, cooking and supply chests work even when the nearby NPC pool is full; nearby enemies do not lock these services. Wilderness storehouses are excluded from the public depot network.

The forge shows the selected result's actual damage/armor, weight, reach, attack delay, strength requirement, penetration, accuracy and equipment bonuses before materials are spent. Crafted recipe equipment also shows its armor/damage and weight. All carried-item lists include equipped items marked `(E)`, including selling and storage. Equipped items are single instances: selling, passing, dropping or depositing them removes the worn piece. Inventory Cross unequips an `(E)` item if there is pack room. Equipped weapons, armor, underlayers and prosthetics contribute their full weight; backpack cargo reductions apply only to carried cargo.

Resale uses 50% of listed item value before local supply, demand and faction modifiers. Weapons and armor have 25% higher base prices and approximately 25% higher weight, rounded to whole units. Regional staple exports retain some profitable transport routes. Injuries progressively reduce carrying capacity, especially chest and arm injuries; healthy strength and equipment bonuses still matter. The fugitive start carries less spare timber so the heavier equipment does not force an overloaded start.

Camp construction again offers farm plots, forges, research benches, palisades, storage and wind pumps, with quoted timber/iron and research requirements. Farming increases harvest yield. Cooking improves ration and food batch yield; recipes now include jerky, oatcakes and stew. Leatherworking is part of Smithing: raw hides and cloth make leather vests or boots at a bench. Engineering reduces construction timber, speeds construction and repairs mechanical limbs. Science reduces the cost of research that unlocks materials, higher forge tiers and camp facilities.

Bed options quote the actual fee. Important settlements charge more; sanctuary, owned-home, clan and field beds retain their applicable free access. Bleeding accumulates more slowly and drains blood more slowly. The world and health screens show consciousness recovery; the resting bar shows current physical recovery (health, blood and fatigue), replacing the fixed five-minute countdown. Untreated wounds still require dressings. Automatic slot-zero saves, including the exit save, are skipped while every living squad member is incapacitated.

Fight stance acquires opponents out to 14m and ignores actors in another interior or on another floor. Explicit attacks work inside large rooms. Choosing Fight cancels a prior block/hold-fire order. The existing weapon reach, movement priority, swing cooldown and physical partitions still apply. Loot options show the percentage remaining, and the loot screen also shows the percentage taken. Taking vital animal meat/hides or a robot's core salvage immediately kills the downed target. Loot history persists through ordinary saves. Rain now uses faint falling drops and small splashes rather than thick uniform diagonal marks.

A long-travel regression also fixed two NPC rescuers claiming the same casualty, which could invalidate a subsequent save.

Save format 35 appends loot/recovery records. Existing format 34 and older expeditions upgrade automatically. Keep your save files when replacing the EBOOT.

Qualification: 54 native suites, genuine pre-1.2.1 save migration, all 30 armor sets, 720 walking poses, eight backpacks, and actual PPSSPP controller scenarios for the changed UI and gameplay. See the included evidence manifest for the final executable hash and exact results. No physical PSP hardware test.
