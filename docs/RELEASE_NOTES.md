# Dust 1.1.0 — The inhabited wilderness

Wilderness travel now has an active encounter director that places threats, camps and road traffic ahead of the player, using actual on-screen activity rather than distant groups as its pacing signal. Small groups fit the limited live actor pool. Off-screen combat can return to background simulation with wounds, members and equipment preserved, freeing space for new encounters. Cleared sites, observed camps, recruits, prisoners, bounties and quest parties are protected.

Starting-region encounters include small groups of weaker bandits. The director mixes raiders, beasts, scavengers, hunters, traders, refugees, salvage crews, wounded travelers and ambush camps. It respects settlement boundaries, terrain, player bases, visible areas and nearby player squads.

Sixteen named abandoned compounds add 48 buildings and structures, including foundries, quarry/pump works, watch posts, shrines, clinics, caravan stops, a border fort and freight yards. Enter their buildings to search finite salvage, read old records or shelter; some have hostile occupants inside. They have varied exteriors, compound arrangements and interior furnishings using the game's existing painted architecture assets. These are small compounds with multiple enterable rooms/buildings, not sprawling dungeon maps.

Greatswords now stay in a ready guard in Fight even without a target. Passive retains the shoulder rest. A Square button symbol appears beside Passive/Fight.

Save format 33 appends persistent sites and encounter pacing, preserving the complete v32 prefix. Existing saves gain the new sites. Keep your save files when installing the new EBOOT.

Native validation covers long analog travel routes, depleted late-game worlds, replenishment after simulated clears, v32 migration, exploration/salvage interactions, interior guards, off-screen combat snapshots and stance toggling. Player health is held constant in the travel tests; those tests measure encounter availability rather than combat balance. Actual PSP executable qualification uses PPSSPP 1.20.4. No physical PSP hardware test.
