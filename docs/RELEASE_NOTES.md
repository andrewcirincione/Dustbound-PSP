# Dust 1.2.0 — Old-world expeditions

Thirty additional named wilderness locations bring the total to 46. The original sixteen sites also gain larger interiors. Across all locations there are 106 explorable floors, with one to three floors per site, connected rooms, machinery halls, reading rooms, barracks, sleeping galleries, side stores and guarded vaults. The camera scrolls with the player. Physical partitions and furniture block movement; walls also block attacks and sight.

Nineteen locations are inhabited: caravan refuges, guild yards, fishing and survey lodges, pilgrim houses, field hospitals, a smuggler exchange and organized faction outposts. Their inhabitants follow faction relations. Occupied sites have guards, a supply counter and resting places. Their supplies are owned goods. Other sites contain scavengers, raiders or old-world automatons.

Six new generated building models are fitted to the isometric ground plane with measured door and foundation coordinates. New generated metal, concrete, slate and ceramic materials distinguish interiors. Two animated robot designs have idle, walking and attacking poses. Dangerous upper floors can contain larger core wardens. Machines yield parts and metal rather than meat.

Enemies and final-floor weapon and armor rewards follow site tier, with additional danger on upper floors. The starting region caps sites at a lower tier. Each floor has finite loot caches. Leaving, returning and saving retain cache depletion, enemy wounds, deaths and looted equipment. Friendly supply counters replenish ordinary consumables daily.

Save format 34 upgrades older saves, including genuine v32 and v33 files with the old 448-building capacity. Existing gear, characters, housing and progress are retained; new sites are added. Building capacity increases to 640. Keep your save files when replacing the EBOOT.

Qualification covers all 106 floors for reachable stairs, caches, records, beds and counters using actual collision, a complete analog-controlled route across a refuge, occupied trading, finite loot, three-floor travel, robot salvage, persistent deaths, historical saves and the existing regression suites. Four 20-minute travel simulations check encounter availability while holding player health constant. Neutral traffic no longer suppresses overdue hostile encounters. Actual PSP executable checks use PPSSPP 1.20.4 for all six new exteriors, representative interiors, analog walking, upstairs/downstairs, third-floor access, trading, salvage and robot combat. No physical PSP hardware test.
