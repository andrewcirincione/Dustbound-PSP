Dust armor recovery — 12 November 2026

Thirty sets were planned in the recovered manifest. Only five source-art sheets
exist: Cinder Scout, Salt Runner, Caravan Leather, Quarry Jack and Road Warden.
No new armor-set implementation, new items, world distribution, PSP build or
release was completed by the earlier pass. Published code remains 1.0.1.

These screenshots are native development previews made through the production
renderer. The review executable temporarily substitutes the five recovered
assets and their planned stats in its own memory. It does not alter saved games
or the release catalog. These are not PPSSPP or physical PSP captures.

The archive includes each complete inventory and world capture at 480x272,
mirrored passive/combat pose reviews, and the exact recovery-status JSON. The original five art sheets and the
30-set planning manifest are preserved on the armor-recovery-review branch.

Why arms look unchanged
The renderer selects a generic cloth sleeve or bare upper arm, plus a generic
bracer or bare forearm. These choices are based on outfit family and armor
value. There are no individual outfit arm meshes/sprites. The recovered
manifest explicitly briefs sleeveless torsos and contains no arm-part assets.
Torso armor nevertheless has 65% arm-hit coverage in combat; hits beyond this
coverage bypass that armor. Both arms were checked at covered and uncovered
rolls for all five preview loadouts.

Original approved shoulder/arm geometry and weapon poses were preserved.
This is a recovery review, not a completed 30-set release.
