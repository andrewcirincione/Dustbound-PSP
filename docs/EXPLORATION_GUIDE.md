# Exploration guide — Dust 1.2.0

The 46 sites include 30 additions and upgrades to the original sixteen. Interiors range from 16–32 by 24–32 world units and one to three floors. Explore on foot; the camera follows. Use Cross > Environment at staircases, caches, records and service counters. Stairs lead to physically separate floors. Square toggles Passive/Fight.

Occupied does not always mean friendly: faction relations still apply. Their supply caches belong to their inhabitants. Traders carry ordinary supplies and restock consumables daily. Ruin loot is finite. Clearing a floor or taking equipment from an enemy persists after leaving and saving.

Listed tiers are authored danger tiers, from 0 (low) to 4 (legendary). Sites within the starting country are capped at tier 1. Upper floors add stronger defenders; final floors contain the best caches for their actual tier. Machines yield salvage rather than meat.
