# Dust field guide — 0.19.0

The 25 chapters below match the in-game guide. ![select](../assets/ui/select.svg) inside a menu opens its contextual chapter.

## 1. Controls

Analog: move. D-pad Up: toggle limb health, blood, food, fatigue and load. Down: skills. Left: worn gear. Right: squad management.

![cross](../assets/ui/cross.svg): interact. ![triangle](../assets/ui/triangle.svg): pack. ![square](../assets/ui/square.svg): fight/passive. Hold L + Square: choose someone to attack (also Start > Squad). ![circle](../assets/ui/circle.svg): sneak. ![l](../assets/ui/l.svg): next squad member. ![r](../assets/ui/r.svg): tactical pause. ![select](../assets/ui/select.svg): world atlas. ![start](../assets/ui/start.svg): pause menu: ![l](../assets/ui/l.svg)/![r](../assets/ui/r.svg) change Pause, Squad and World tabs. D-pad navigates menus.

Menus pause time. ![select](../assets/ui/select.svg) opens help. ![l](../assets/ui/l.svg)/![r](../assets/ui/r.svg) switch members or guide pages. Cross opens nearby actions. Pause with R, choose an action to queue it, then R resumes. Four actions can wait; moving cancels them. Analog movement cancels work. Map pins never move you.

## 2. Your Drifter

New expedition opens character creation. Choose a name, origin, species, body, skin, hairstyle, hair color and beard. ![horizontal](../assets/ui/horizontal.svg) adjusts each choice. ![cross](../assets/ui/cross.svg) edits your name or begins.

Origins change equipment, supplies and skills. Outlaws begin with a bounty. Caravan starts as a group of three. Species changes health, carry, speed, appetite and starting skills. The preview shows your starting values. Clockworks need spare parts, not food or bandages. A town barber can change your appearance later.

## 3. The Borderlands

The 1536 by 1536 borderlands contain nineteen settlements and nine terrain regions. Roads connect towns. Rivers have fords near roads; hills rise into mesas and snowfields.

![select](../assets/ui/select.svg) opens the atlas. D-pad selects a known town; ![l](../assets/ui/l.svg)/![r](../assets/ui/r.svg) changes pages. The blue triangle is you; white squares are towns. World activity records last-seen sightings, not live tracking. ![cross](../assets/ui/cross.svg) places a reference pin. ![triangle](../assets/ui/triangle.svg) opens the local view. ![l](../assets/ui/l.svg)/![r](../assets/ui/r.svg) selects known towns. ![square](../assets/ui/square.svg) reveals surveyed geography after buying a chart. Town ownership, prosperity and unrest change with world events. Camps and depleted deposits persist.

## 4. Combat

![square](../assets/ui/square.svg) toggles fighting. L + Square opens opponents; Cross orders an attack, including neutrals (assault adds bounty). Small feet marks: teal line friendly, gold dot neutral, red chevron hostile. Red corners mark your opponent. Fight stance closes to weapon reach and attacks at cooldown. The analog stick overrides pursuit. Followers engage nearby threats. ![r](../assets/ui/r.svg) pauses combat for orders. Heavy steel handles best with strength equal to twice its weight. Weak or injured arms lengthen preparation and recovery. Mauls pierce armor; glaives reach farther; greatswords hit hard; anchor cleavers bleed. Guns use cartridges; bows use bolts.

Armor protects specific body locations. Piercing weapons ignore some armor; serrated blades bleed; staggering hits interrupt. Skills improve through use. Retreat from stronger opponents and use terrain. Gold windup marks show incoming strikes. Enemy combat bars show remaining knockout reserve from head, chest and blood. Square to passive cancels your attack; move away to disengage.

## 5. Wounds & Rescue

Head or chest injuries and blood loss can knock a survivor out. Severe injuries can cause a recovery coma. After stabilizing, a survivor wakes prone and can give themselves first aid. Critical injuries may leave them crawling. Severe head trauma or blood loss can kill.

Dress an injured body part before it can heal. Clotting stops blood loss. It still takes a dressing to start healing. Severe wounds take longer to bandage and recover. Beds and clinic care speed up recovery. Health shows healthy tissue in teal, untreated damage in red and dressed damage in gold. Nearby Actions offers first aid and emergency dressings. First aid stops bleeding before treating each wound. Check the remaining charge on a field dressing. An ally can treat someone who is down or carry them to an unoccupied bed. A rare passerby may help or carry you to a safe bed. Lost limbs never regrow. A bad leg injury makes you limp; a missing leg or two disabled legs makes you crawl. One missing arm penalizes two-handed weapons. Health: R opens individual limb slots. Fit replacements for 2 machine parts. Surgeon Venn in Dusk Haven removes limbs for 400 silver. Copper Cross sells reinforced limbs; Iron Gate precision limbs; Black Mesa relic limbs. Prosthetics are uncommon on ordinary travelers; high-grade parts are rarer. Actual fitted limbs on fallen wanderers can be looted. Death is permanent; dismiss the body from Squad to free its slot.

## 6. Food & Sleep

Food is eaten below forty hunger. You eat what you need and save the remainder. Supplies come from your pack, then a nearby companion or owned depot within three meters. Water supports farms; tonics restore blood and treat exposure. Starvation damages the chest.

An inn bed costs 25 silver and starts safe rest. Camp shelters are free. Six roadside wayfarer beds have finite caches. Rock passes provide cover and outer bypasses. Sleeping reduces fatigue and helps dressed wounds heal. Severe hunger slows recovery. Move to wake. The sun completes a day in thirty minutes of simulation; menus and tactical pause stop time.

## 7. Equipment

Six slots: weapon, torso, head, boots, legs, back. Pack ![cross](../assets/ui/cross.svg) equips; ![square](../assets/ui/square.svg) passes one to an ally; ![triangle](../assets/ui/triangle.svg) switches carried/worn gear. ![cross](../assets/ui/cross.svg) on worn gear removes it if space allows. Hovering equipment changes the single character preview and compares stats. WORN DISGUISE shows your current outfit; PREVIEW shows the hovered outfit. Equipping commits it.

Plate protects more but slows movement and hinders stealth. Cloth is light; travel gear favors weather or movement. Higher grades improve the same role. Torso armor covers chest/arms; headgear covers head; leg armor and boots have separate coverage chances. MOVE% is the worn speed bonus. Weight above capacity slows you further.

## 8. Merchants

Talk to a trader and choose Trade. ![horizontal](../assets/ui/horizontal.svg) switches buying and selling; ![l](../assets/ui/l.svg)/![r](../assets/ui/r.svg) filters supplies, weapons, armor or backpacks. ![cross](../assets/ui/cross.svg) trades one item. Shops have finite stocks and refresh daily with different gear.

Regional exports are cheap; imports and shortages cost more. Green percentages mean a good buy or sale; red means poor, relative to ordinary buy/resale value. Ask merchants for trading tips. Hostile factions refuse trade unless fooled by a disguise. Stolen goods appear in red. The selling list shows the chance a trader accepts the item without reporting you. Stealth and Thievery improve the odds. Black Mesa fences most outside goods. The item details show its stats and effects before purchase.

## 9. Forging Gear

Enter a smithy and walk to the forge. Choose slot, design, material and quality with ![horizontal](../assets/ui/horizontal.svg). The preview updates before you spend anything.

![cross](../assets/ui/cross.svg) on Forge makes the selected design. Metal, cloth and timber costs scale with material and grade; relic gear also needs a relic. Higher grades require smithing experience and research. Missing materials or a full pack cancel the transaction without charging you.

## 10. Crafting

Crafting recipes make supplies, refined metal, ammunition and prosthetics. A forge smelts ore and shapes weapons. A campfire cooks grain or meat with water. A research bench makes dressings.

Stand close to the station. Enter a smithy before using its forge. Each ingredient shows HAVE/NEED: 6/3 means you own six and need three. Green means enough, including exact amounts; red means short. Research, station reach and fuel are also shown. ![square](../assets/ui/square.svg) switches recipes/equipment. Research consumes relics and silver in three tiers. Skills rise when crafting succeeds. A shared depot supplies worker jobs, but hand crafting uses the selected survivor's pack.

## 11. Your Outpost

Build outside settlement boundaries. Choose a structure and place it beside the selected character. Buildings cannot overlap; materials are charged only after validation.

Shelters heal; farms need water and yield grain; wind pumps irrigate nearby crops. Forges and benches unlock production. Depots hold sixteen clean stacks. ![cross](../assets/ui/cross.svg) opens storage; ![horizontal](../assets/ui/horizontal.svg) switches sides; ![square](../assets/ui/square.svg) moves a full stack. Camps attract raiders, so keep medicine and a retreat route.

## 12. Squad Jobs

D-pad Right opens Squad management. ![l](../assets/ui/l.svg)/![r](../assets/ui/r.svg) chooses a member; ![vertical](../assets/ui/vertical.svg) selects a row; ![horizontal](../assets/ui/horizontal.svg) changes its value. Follow trails the assigned group leader; Hold stays put. Mine gathers local deposits; Farm waters and harvests; Medic treats injured allies.

Smelt and Cook use nearby stations and can collect ingredients from a nearby depot. Workers fetch water and ingredients, walk to stations, and store output; cooks keep two rations. Haul deposits raw cargo. Job status explains delays. Select a worker and move to interrupt their current action. Up to eight living members may join.

## 13. Crime & Stealth

![circle](../assets/ui/circle.svg) sneaks. Detection depends on stealth skill, equipment, distance and weather. Sneak and ![cross](../assets/ui/cross.svg) near a civilian to inspect a pickpocket attempt. Select an item with ![vertical](../assets/ui/vertical.svg). Risk reflects weight, value, approach and visible witnesses. Getting behind the target improves odds. ![cross](../assets/ui/cross.svg) steals one; ![circle](../assets/ui/circle.svg) cancels. Owned salvage says STEAL. Locks take time to pick; tools are optional.

Shop and house chests are private. Repeated theft can bring pocket checks for two days. Witnesses can warn you or raise a bounty. A theft can fail, drawing guards. Stolen stacks retain an owner until fenced. Review the charges with a watch officer before confirming a payment. Jail staff treat injuries while you serve time. Failed lockpicks train your skill. Nearby Actions frees other prisoners or slaves. Work takes time and guards can catch you. Some freed people flee; others follow independently. Only Recruit adds them to your squad. Leave me alone dismisses a follower.

## 14. Factions

Compact guards roads and wells; Rust Caravans trade salvage. Shogunate markets sell lamellar and glaives. Ash monks sell medicine; Black Mesa fences stolen goods. Vultures raid; wild packs hunt.

Contracts raise standing; theft lowers it. The Factions ledger shows each faction’s outstanding amount and charges. Ash Monastery, Shogun Rest and Vulture Nest require matching body armor and headwear; close inspections can expose a disguise. Hired guards cost 240 silver for escort or 180 for camp duty: one simulated day. They fight and bandage independently, keep their gear, wait outside interiors and return home on expiry.

## 15. Contracts

Ask merchants or marshals for work. Freight needs a refundable deposit and four goods. Bring the cargo to the receiving contact. Caravans wait for you outside town. Square on the Journal entry locates the caravan. Escort or patrol work pays 12 silver per 30 seconds of nearby movement, plus a completion bonus. Stay within 8m. A wanted target has a silver mark beside their name. Their loot screen lists the paying factions. Carry them to a paying jailkeeper. A live target pays twice as much.

Journal shows your objective, payment and contact. Completed contracts have their own tab.

Read wanted posters by a jail door or ask a jailkeeper for the warrants. Cross tracks or drops a case. Square pins its last reported location. Triangle in the Journal opens tracked warrants, and left or right switches to paid cases. Nine Knives, Red Surveyor and Hollow Prince each have a standing warrant. Their deaths are permanent.

At a house for sale, open the deed from Nearby Actions. Once you own the house, enter and choose Furnish house. Left or right picks a spot, up or down picks furniture, Cross places it, and Square removes it. Approach a chest to open the shared stash. Left or right switches between your pack and the stash. Cross moves one item and Square moves the selected stack.

## 16. Saves

Six manual slots plus autosave. ![start](../assets/ui/start.svg), Save or Load opens a ledger with name, day, party size and silver. Choose a slot; ![cross](../assets/ui/cross.svg) asks for confirmation. Save-and-title opens the ledger; choose and confirm a slot.

Saves use version, size and checksum validation and preserve a previous .bak file. Invalid files never replace your running expedition. Older saves through 0.11.0 remain loadable. Back them up before overwriting. Autosave is slot zero and is never used for manual saving.

## 17. Weather

Dust, rain, acid mist and snow change visibility, terrain colors and survival pressure. Storms reduce detection and ranged accuracy. Acid and cold build exposure outdoors; shelters and sealed gear protect you.

Tonics and rest help recover. Rain irrigates farms. The northern highlands are colder; the fen produces acid mist. Weather changes as time passes. The world ledger reports storms and settlement events so you can plan supplies and routes.

## 18. Captivity

For a minor bounty the watch asks you to pay or serve time. At 100 silver or more they pursue and arrest you when you fall. Jail staff treat injuries and seize stolen goods. In the cell, ![cross](../assets/ui/cross.svg) offers bail or lockpicking. The watch can relock an open cell if you linger. Move to the exit and press ![cross](../assets/ui/cross.svg) to leave. Escaping adds a charge and the watch can pursue you.

Slavers carry fallen survivors to a work camp. Shackled prisoners work in issued garb. The camp chest holds their seized equipment and supplies. Press ![cross](../assets/ui/cross.svg) to pick your shackles. Former captors can recognize escapees.

## 19. Building Interiors

Approach a house, inn, shop, tower or workshop door and press ![cross](../assets/ui/cross.svg) to enter. Walk around the room with the analog stick. Furniture blocks movement; use the aisles. ![cross](../assets/ui/cross.svg) near the door returns outside.

Counters open local trade and smithies sell weapons and armor. Inns offer beds for 25 silver each. Owned beds are free. Join the Dustward Clan for 280 silver to use beds and training dummies at any of its lodges. Dummies train your equipped weapon skill or Martial Arts up to level 20. Use a research bench directly from outside. Indoor time passes: hunger, bleeding and healing continue. Nearby followers in your group enter with you. Other groups and hired guards remain outside.

Save games preserve the building and indoor position. Switching squad members leaves the room. Interiors shelter you from weather; occupants who distrust you may demand you leave and attack if you stay.

## 20. Backpacks

Buy travel packs, medicine rolls, ore harnesses and bedroll packs from the BACKPACKS shop tab. Preview before buying. Other back gear rotates daily.

CARGO: travel 80%, medicine 85%, ore 60%, bedroll 70% of carried weight. Worn gear and carried people keep full weight. CARRY adds load allowance; LOAD previews a swap. All packs use the same 24 inventory slots.

FIGHT reduces melee attack/defence: travel -2, medicine 0, ore -7, bedroll -4. Bulky packs hurt stealth. Medicine rolls speed up first aid. Bedroll packs help you recover while resting. Buy a Bedroll in Supplies to lay out a portable bed.

## 21. Finding Work

Work is posted intermittently. Ask a merchant, marshal, innkeeper or smith, or ask locals what they have heard. If someone is hiring, the local contact will name the job. A town can have zero, one or two postings. After accepting one, allow time for new work. The Journal lists the objective, payout and the person who pays.

## 22. Orders & Groups

Squad management sets Balanced, Defensive, Keep range or Hold fire. Defensive gives +18 defence but never attacks. Ranged allies retreat if a foe closes within 3.5m. Projectiles travel and hit walls or allies: clear your firing line.

Assign each survivor to one of three groups and choose its leader. A pin marks the destination on both maps. Follow its line on the minimap. Walk with the analog stick; group members follow their leader. Four job priorities choose the first available task. Put Follow or Hold last. Manual movement pauses a queue; resume the Priority queue in Squad management.

## 23. Camp Work & Fuel

The wilderness has persistent camps, patrols, refugees, raiders, beasts, prisoners and armies. Groups travel, rest, gather and clash even far away. Camp beds are free when unoccupied. Ore seams and cuttable timber have distinct markings and sit outside settlements. Campfires cook 2 meat into 1 ration. Road traders and some smugglers carry their own stock. Search a chest to see its contents before taking anything.

Highlight a Bedroll in the pack and press ![cross](../assets/ui/cross.svg) to lay it out. Use Nearby Actions to rest or pack it up. Construction consumes materials when a foundation is placed. Stay beside it to build; moving cancels work. Resume from the nearby action or Camp ledger. Unfinished stations and beds cannot work.

Campfires and forges burn one charge per production action. Add clean timber for four charges or coal for ten. Stations lose a little condition with use. One timber repairs 35 condition. Town workshops supply their own fuel. Windpumps irrigate nearby farms. Queues skip unavailable jobs.

## 24. Trauma & Repair

Cuts can worsen while bleeding. Bandaging starts gradual recovery from organic damage. Deep wounds take longer. Rest in a bed to recover faster.

Mechanical characters and prosthetics need timed repairs with spare parts. Medicine and sleep cannot repair metal. Health & recovery shows health, trauma and armor condition. Cloth restores equipment condition.

Free allies can pick a captive's shackles. First aid, trade and completed work improve faction standing.

## 25. Armor Layers

Use Armor Layers to fit a light body garment under outer armor. Weight must be five or less. Swaps preserve stolen ownership and fail safely if your pack is full.

Outer body covers 95% of chest hits and 65% of arm hits; headwear covers 85%, legwear 90%, boots 35%. An uncovered hit bypasses that piece. Blunt impacts use 72% of outer protection. Underlayers add reduced protection. Wear lowers protection to a minimum of 65%; maintain with cloth. Inspect both armor and movement before equipping.

## Nearby identities and sightings

Read equipment and formation to judge a group. Nearby labels show role, faction color and hostility. Start > World > Factions shows the color key and your standing. Hidden ambushers remain hidden until detected. Combat bars show remaining knockout reserve; no skill readouts appear from proximity.

World activity records last-seen places and times. Cross locates a report on the map; Cross there sets a manual pin. Councils show named claimants and policies before a pledge.
