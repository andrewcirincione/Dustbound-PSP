# Dustbound field guide — 0.2.0

This guide mirrors the eighteen chapters available in-game. Select inside a menu opens its contextual chapter.

## 1. Controls

D-pad or analog: move in screen direction. X: nearby interaction. Triangle: pack. Square: fight/passive. Circle: sneak. L: next squad member. R: tactical pause. Select: world atlas. Start: expedition menu.

Menus pause the world. Select inside a menu opens its guide. L/R switch inventory tabs or guide pages. Move to cancel a work order or travel route.

## 2. Your Drifter

New expedition opens character creation. Choose a name, origin, ancestry, body, skin, hairstyle, hair color, beard and scars. Left/right adjusts each choice; X edits your name or begins.

Origins change equipment, supplies and skills. Outlaws begin with a bounty. Caravan starts three companions. Ancestry changes starting strengths. A town barber can change your appearance later.

## 3. The Borderlands

The 384 by 384 borderlands contain sixteen settlements and nine terrain regions. Roads connect towns. Rivers have fords near roads; hills rise into mesas and snowfields.

Select opens the atlas. Move the cursor; X orders travel. L/R selects known towns. Square reveals surveyed geography after buying a chart. Town ownership, prosperity and unrest change with world events. Camps and depleted deposits persist.

## 4. Combat

Square toggles fighting. Move into weapon reach to attack automatically; followers engage nearby threats. R pauses combat for orders. Heavy weapons hit harder but recover slowly. Guns use cartridges; bows use bolts.

Armor protects specific body locations. Piercing weapons ignore some armor; serrated blades bleed; staggering hits interrupt. Skills improve through use. Retreat from stronger opponents and use terrain.

## 5. Wounds & Rescue

Six body parts, blood and fatigue are separate. Head or chest failure knocks a survivor out. Bleeding can kill an unconscious character. Triangle, Body, X treats the selected survivor; X near an injured ally treats or carries them.

Dressings stop bleeding and restore some health. Sleeping restores the rest. A doctor charges for full treatment. Lost limbs require a mechanical replacement. Death is permanent; dismiss the body from Squad to free its slot.

## 6. Food & Sleep

Rations are eaten automatically below forty hunger. Carry food on each squad member. Water supports farms; tonics restore blood and treat exposure. Starvation damages the chest.

An inn bed costs 25 silver and starts safe rest. Camp shelters are free. Sleeping reduces fatigue and restores wounds once bleeding stops. Move to wake. The sun completes a day in thirty minutes of simulation; menus and tactical pause stop time.

## 7. Equipment

Six visible slots: weapon, torso, head, boots, legs and back. The catalog has 2,016 combinations across 84 silhouettes, six materials and four quality grades. Every variant has visible material/quality details and mechanical differences.

Pack X equips or uses; Square passes one item to an ally. Triangle switches between carried items and worn equipment. Inspect shows damage, armor and effects; X on worn gear removes it if space allows. Weight above capacity slows movement.

## 8. Merchants

Talk to a trader and choose Trade. Left/right switches buying and selling; L/R filters supplies, weapons or armor. X trades one item. Shops have finite stocks and refresh daily with different gear.

Prices depend on quality, faction relations, local prosperity. Hostile factions refuse trade. Selling stolen goods back to their owner can expose you; Black Mesa fences accept them. The item details show its stats and effects before purchase.

## 9. Forging Gear

Visit a forge, then choose Gear forge from the expedition menu or smith dialogue. Choose slot, design, material and quality with left/right. The preview updates before you spend anything.

X on Forge makes the selected design. Metal, cloth and timber costs scale with material and grade; relic gear also needs a relic. Higher grades require smithing experience and research. Missing materials or a full pack cancel the transaction without charging you.

## 10. Crafting

Crafting recipes make supplies, refined metal, ammunition and prosthetics. A forge smelts ore and shapes weapons. A campfire cooks grain or meat with water. A research bench makes dressings.

Stand close to the station. The recipe screen lists costs and unlocks. Research consumes relics and silver in three tiers. Skills rise when crafting succeeds. A shared depot supplies worker jobs, but hand crafting uses the selected survivor's pack.

## 11. Your Outpost

Build outside settlement boundaries. Choose a structure and place it beside the selected character. Buildings cannot overlap; materials are charged only after validation.

Shelters heal; farms need water and yield grain; wind pumps irrigate nearby crops. Forges and benches unlock production. Depots hold sixteen item stacks of any type. X opens storage; left/right switches deposit/withdraw. Camps attract raiders, so keep medicine and a retreat route.

## 12. Squad Jobs

Triangle, Squad: choose a survivor with left/right, then assign a job with X. Follow trails the selected leader; Hold stays put. Mine gathers local deposits; Farm waters and harvests; Medic treats injured allies.

Smelt and Cook use nearby stations and can collect ingredients from a nearby depot. Haul moves pack resources into storage. Each worker needs supplies and food. Select a worker and move to interrupt their current action. Up to eight living members may join.

## 13. Crime & Stealth

Circle sneaks. Detection depends on stealth skill, equipment, distance and weather. Sneak and X near a civilian to attempt pickpocketing. Owned salvage also counts as theft; locked containers require tools and time.

Witnesses create a faction penalty and bounty. A theft can fail, drawing guards. Stolen stacks retain an owner until fenced. Paying a bounty requires a town official. Guards may imprison a defeated wanted survivor; breaking out or paying bail is possible.

## 14. Factions

The Frontier Compact guards the old roads. Rust Caravans trade salvage. The Iron Shogunate rules the east; Ash monks shelter pilgrims. Black Mesa fences stolen goods. Red Vultures raid; wild packs hunt.

Completed contracts improve standing. Theft worsens it. The Factions ledger shows standing and controlled towns. Settlement unrest affects prosperity and prices. Fighting a faction can close its shops; peaceful jobs offer a way back.

## 15. Contracts

Town residents offer delivery, supply, hunt and relic contracts. Accept through dialogue; track objectives in the Journal. Deliveries require reaching another town. Supplies require the listed items. Hunts count victories after acceptance.

Return to the issuing settlement and claim in the Journal with X. Rewards grant silver, reputation and experience. Contracts persist in saves. Complete contracts, research relic technology and survey the frontier to advance the expedition legacy.

## 16. Saves

Six manual slots plus autosave. Start, Save or Load opens a ledger with name, day, party size and silver. Choose a slot; X asks for confirmation. Save-and-title uses the selected manual slot.

Saves use version, size and checksum validation and preserve a previous .bak file. Invalid files never replace your running expedition. Version 0.1 saves migrate on load. Back them up before overwriting. Autosave is slot zero and is never used for manual saving.

## 17. Weather

Dust, rain, acid mist and snow change visibility, terrain colors and survival pressure. Storms reduce detection and ranged accuracy. Acid and cold build exposure outdoors; shelters and sealed gear protect you.

Tonics and rest help recover. Rain irrigates farms. The northern highlands are colder; the fen produces acid mist. Weather changes as time passes. The world ledger reports storms and settlement events so you can plan supplies and routes.

## 18. Captivity

Wanted survivors defeated by lawful guards may wake in town custody. They lose stolen cargo and must serve time, pay bail or attempt an escape. A conscious free companion can still be selected.

When imprisoned, movement is restricted to the holding area. X opens the custody dialogue. Paying clears the local bounty. Escape attempts use lockpicking, may fail and raise the bounty. The prison timer, injuries and inventory persist through saving.
