# Dust 1.0.3: one hundred independently drawn weapons

All 38 existing weapon types receive two or three newly drawn models, for exactly 100 added weapons. Eight are legendary. Names, geometry, materials, weight, attack cadence, damage, penetration, accuracy and value vary. Four plain utilitarian constructions remain; the other designs use distinctive ceramic, glass, bone, metal, enamel, lacquer and industrial structures.

## Distribution and smithies

Living opponents and new encounters draw equipment appropriate to their strength and weapon type. Weak enemies receive salvaged/field weapons, veterans receive military/master constructions, and exceptional enemies can carry the best designs. Existing named trophies retain their owner and item identity while receiving their new drawn construction and matching high-end stats. Dead, stripped, imprisoned, recruited and player-owned characters are protected during the one-time migration.

Each smith stocks a combined selection of weapons and armor. Local settlements have 15 options, hubs 25, fortresses 35, and capitals 45, with approximately three weapons for every two armor pieces. Items rotate at the beginning of each day. Inventory remains depleted after purchases and reloads during the same day. Better constructions follow settlement importance; legendary weapons only enter capital stock, at a low rate. Regional faction designs are favored. Separate armorers receive a similarly sized armor-only selection.

## Actual held models

Each of the 100 drawings has a primary hand anchor and, where relevant, a separately calibrated supporting grip. Firearms align their muzzle with aim and put the primary hand near the trigger, rather than on the buttstock. Bows use the arrow's direction; their limbs span across the aiming axis. Cutting edges keep their intended parity through left/right reflection. Extra construction details are part of the painted weapon, with clear grip areas and hand clearance.

Qualification includes distinct-silhouette checks, rotated/mirrored grip raster checks, anatomy and supporting-hand checks, 200 production-renderer equipped/contact reviews, old-save migration, 15–45 daily stock tests, PSP compilation and controller-driven PPSSPP checks for the complete new catalog. Physical PSP hardware and audible output are not measured by these checks.

Save format 31 appends the weapon state and reads existing format-30 and earlier saves without renumbering existing items.
