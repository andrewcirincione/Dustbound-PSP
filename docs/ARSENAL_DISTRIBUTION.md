# Dust 1.0.4: 120 independently drawn expansion weapons

The original 100 additions cover all 38 weapon types. A further 20 greatswords bring the expansion catalog to 120 weapons, including 23 greatswords and 12 legendary weapons overall. The new greatswords use five equipment tiers, varied blade silhouettes and guards, and long usable grips in bone, horn, enamel, metal, ceramic and stone.

## Distribution and smithies

Living opponents and new encounters draw equipment appropriate to their strength and weapon type. Weak enemies receive salvaged/field weapons, veterans receive military/master constructions, and exceptional enemies can carry the best designs. Existing named trophies retain their owner and item identity while receiving their new drawn construction and matching high-end stats. Dead, stripped, imprisoned, recruited and player-owned characters are protected during the one-time migration.

Each daily smith refresh includes one new greatsword (two in fortresses/capitals), within its existing stock budget. Each smith stocks a combined selection of weapons and armor. Local settlements have 15 options, hubs 25, fortresses 35, and capitals 45, with approximately three weapons for every two armor pieces. Items rotate at the beginning of each day. Inventory remains depleted after purchases and reloads during the same day. Better constructions follow settlement importance; legendary weapons only enter capital stock, at a low rate. Regional faction designs are favored. Separate armorers receive a similarly sized armor-only selection.

## Actual held models

Each of the 120 drawings has a primary hand anchor and, where relevant, a separately calibrated supporting grip. Firearms align their muzzle with aim and put the primary hand near the trigger, rather than on the buttstock. Bows use the arrow's direction; their limbs span across the aiming axis. Cutting edges keep their intended parity through left/right reflection. Extra construction details are part of the painted weapon, with clear grip areas and hand clearance.

Qualification includes distinct-silhouette checks, rotated/mirrored grip raster checks, anatomy and supporting-hand checks, 240 production-renderer equipped/contact reviews, old-save migration, 15–45 daily stock tests, PSP compilation and controller-driven PPSSPP checks for the complete new catalog. Physical PSP hardware and audible output are not measured by these checks.

Save format 32 appends the greatsword state and reads existing format-31 and earlier saves without renumbering existing items.
