# Dust 1.0.2 armor distribution

Thirty generated sets (fifteen heavy) now appear as real body, head, leg and boot items. Heavy sets trade speed and stealth for protection and weight. Arm coverage follows each set's protection, and the fitted shoulders and trousers are preserved.

## World rules

- Ordinary raiders carry zero to two scavenged, mixed pieces from low-grade Bone Scavenger and Iron Labourer sets. Veteran raiders combine stronger Red Reaver or outlaw plates with poorer accessories. Starving groups retain their ragged clothing and poor weapons. Raider armor upkeep starts below military upkeep.
- Union ranks wear Road Warden, Brass Sentinel and Charter Marshal. Shogunate ranks wear Reed Ashigaru, Gate Lamellar and Steel Retainer. Monastic ranks wear Ember Guard, Censor Carapace and Winter Sohei. Ranged crews use faction scout headwear and boots with their rank's body protection.
- Other travelers draw from regional and independent sets according to their level. Service staff keep their work clothing. Refugees, prisoners, animals and the player's squad retain their special rules.
- Hollow Prince wears Sunken Crown; Abbess Ember wears Cinder Reliquary; Red Surveyor wears Wraith of Salt; Gatewarden Yara wears Last Bastion. Their body pieces retain their existing unique named-item identities. Other important named NPCs retain their authored signature armor.
- Two remote ruined-shop chests contain individual legendary accessories. These chests do not replenish. High-tier salvage and lost-cache encounters have a 1-in-32 roll for a legendary accessory; lower-tier caches cannot roll one. Failed pickup attempts keep the same cache roll.
- Smith and armorer stock follows local faction and market size. Capital smiths or armorers occasionally stock one expensive legendary accessory. Legendary body pieces are never placed into ordinary shop or cache pools. Prices account for armor, weight and rarity; legendary accessories start above 12,000 silver before regional market modifiers.

## Saves and qualification

Save format 30 accepts existing format-29 saves. A one-time armor migration upgrades eligible living NPCs and stored army reserves; player equipment, dead/knocked-out/stripped actors and special service roles are protected. Claimed caches and looted elites stay empty after ordinary save/load.

Qualification requires every native suite, PSP package and embedded-asset checks, all thirty loadouts through PPSSPP button-driven save loading, and 153 production-renderer reviews. Physical PSP hardware and audible output are not measured by these checks.
