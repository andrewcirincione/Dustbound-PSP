# Three Banners

The war uses the established Frontier Compact, Iron Shogunate and Ash Monastery. Traders, outlaws, Vultures and independent travelers remain in the world. The player starts independent. The original sixteen places retain their positions.

| Faction | New capital and starting holdings | Origins and daily life | Visual and military language |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Frontier Compact | Brass Junction, Dusk Haven, Saltwatch, Red Gulch, Cinder Port | Pump engineers and road crews pooled water rights after the reservoirs failed. Elected wardens levy tolls and sentence captives to public works. | Open rail yard, shops, forge and well. Ochre pennants, dust coats, broad hats, rifles and greatswords. |

The three capitals use the existing southern and eastern frontier coordinates. New roads connect them to older settlements, fords give marching armies routes across the rivers, and the atlas marks discovered capitals and shows their current flag. Cultural layouts survive occupation. Compact markets discount supplies, the Shogunate charges an equipment levy, and the Ash hospice offers free beds while the Ash order controls the abbey.

Each power has a standing expedition in the persistent population. It chooses a rival objective from the territory and resources that exist at the time. Marching forces can meet opposing patrols and armies on the road. A successful siege requires a standing attacker and a defeated garrison. Attack intervals and an occupation grace period give the new government time to settle. Existing succession and regional disputes still run.

Conquerors may disarm and shackle up to two living rival defenders in a settlement work yard. The actual captured actors keep their identity and injuries. Guarded, conscious prisoners perform labor for the occupier. Nearby Actions uses the existing restraint rescue system. A change of government releases the prisoners of the previous regime.

Road talk reports real, nearby raiders and recorded military events. Distance delays news, the speaker can frame a victory as their own side's win or loss, and reports expire. The player can also ask ordinary NPCs what they have heard. Reports describe events and directions without adding moving enemies to the atlas.

Version 17 widens the town, building and population arrays. Older saves copy their original fields into the corresponding ranges, retain their owners, inventory and weapon XP, and populate the newly opened frontier once.
