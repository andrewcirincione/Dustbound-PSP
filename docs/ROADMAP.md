# Dustbound development ledger

Original PSP isometric squad survival RPG inspired by Kenshi. No Kenshi code, map, characters, or extracted assets are used. This is a new game, not a port. The requested full Kenshi feature set is the long-term scope; the first milestone is a playable, tested foundation.

## Milestone 0.1 — playable survival sandbox

- Persistent 128 × 128 overworld with five settlements, regional terrain, roads, ruins, resources and wandering factions.
- Up to four controllable squad members, following/holding and resource/medical jobs.
- Real-time combat with tactical pause, ranged and melee equipment, body-part damage, bleeding, knockout, death, carrying and first aid.
- Twenty use-based skills; inventory, weight, hunger, rest and visible equipment layers.
- Trade with finite stocks, recruiting, looting, stealing and lockpicking.
- Camp construction, crafting, farming, research, raids and save slots.
- PSP EBOOT plus reproducible native tests and emulator evidence.

## Future milestones — not claimed in 0.1

- Full navigation mesh and long-distance squad order planning.
- Large-scale faction wars, town takeovers and reactive diplomacy.
- Deep slavery/capture/prison systems, bounties across factions and disguise checks.
- Full Kenshi-scale species, animations, weapon stances, dozens of armor slots and procedural equipment quality.
- Rich interiors, dialogue trees, narrative world states, weather hazards and a larger authored world.
- Large squads and multi-squad management, industrial job chains and construction placement editing.
- Hardware performance and suspend/resume qualification on multiple PSP models.

Only checked, documented release features should be described as implemented. Emulator screenshots must come from the packaged EBOOT. Hardware testing must be reported separately.
